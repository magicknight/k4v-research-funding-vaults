# Commercial relationship disclosure

The maintainer also studies a proposed commercial pure-meme token called K4V,
whose attention thesis is associated with his unfinished K4 scientific work.
The proposed K4V allocation is 30% founder, 50% purpose-bound K4 research
treasury, 12% genesis/public distribution, and 8% disclosed liquidity.

K4V is a disclosed case study, not a condition of using this repository.
Users do not need to acquire K4V, accept the K4 scientific thesis, or accept
any market thesis. This repository does not promise token utility, governance,
IP, revenue, redemption, buybacks, liquidity, appreciation, or returns.

Any grant for this repository would fund reusable open-source infrastructure.
It would not buy K4V, seed liquidity, pay market makers, create artificial
demand, finance token promotion, certify K4, or substitute for issuer,
jurisdictional, tax, sanctions, or transaction-specific legal review.

Because a commercial case study exists, a funder may classify the proposal as
a standard public-good grant, a convertible grant, or out of scope. Those
outcomes should be decided explicitly rather than hidden by presentation.
